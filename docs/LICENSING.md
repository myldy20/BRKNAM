GNU GPL already requires preservation of copyright and license notices. BRKNAM additionally uses the limited origin-attribution mechanism permitted by GNU GPLv3 section 7(b). The exact reasonable notice is defined in `NOTICE` and is intended for an About, Credits, documentation, or equivalent normally visible location, not advertising or generated audio.
