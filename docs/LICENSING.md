# Licensing policy

BRKNAM code is released under `GPL-3.0-or-later`.

## Author credit

GNU GPL already requires preservation of copyright and license notices. BRKNAM additionally uses the limited author-attribution mechanism permitted by GNU GPLv3 section 7(b). The exact reasonable notice is defined in `ATTRIBUTION.md` and is intended for the application's About/Legal view, not advertising or generated audio.

Do not add broader requirements such as mandatory social-media credit, citation in recordings, endorsement, or unchanged branding. Those would exceed the project's attribution intent and may create GPL compatibility problems.

## Contributions

Unless explicitly agreed otherwise, contributions are accepted under `GPL-3.0-or-later` and may be redistributed as part of BRKNAM under that license and the existing section 7(b) attribution term. Contributors retain copyright in their contributions.

Every original source file should include:

```text
SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-3.0-or-later
Copyright (C) YEAR COPYRIGHT-HOLDER
See ATTRIBUTION.md for the GPLv3 section 7(b) attribution term.
```

Third-party files retain their original headers and licenses and must be listed in `THIRD_PARTY.md` when included in a distributed build.
