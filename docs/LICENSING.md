GNU GPL already requires preservation of copyright and license notices. BRKNAM additionally uses the limited origin-attribution mechanism permitted by GNU GPLv3 section 7(b). The exact reasonable notice is defined in [`NOTICE`](../NOTICE):

> Based on BRKNAM by Myldy design — https://github.com/myldy20/BRKNAM

The notice belongs in the application's About/Legal view, or in README/documentation for a source-only or command-line derivative. It is not an advertising clause and does not apply to generated audio.

Do not add broader requirements such as mandatory social-media credit, citation in recordings, endorsement, or unchanged branding. Those exceed the project's attribution intent and may create GPL compatibility problems.

Every original source file should include:


Third-party files retain their original headers and licenses and must be listed in `THIRD_PARTY.md` when included in a distributed build.
